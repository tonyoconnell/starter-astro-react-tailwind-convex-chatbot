# Company Context Workshop Task

This task guides a comprehensive workshop to uncover and articulate your company's authentic brand identity, including brand voice, unique mechanism, and core value propositions.

## Purpose

Facilitate deep discovery of:

- Authentic brand voice (3-5 defining adjectives)
- Unique mechanism (your special approach/method)
- Core value propositions
- Brand story elements
- Differentiation factors

## Prerequisites

Before starting the workshop:

1. 30-60 minutes of uninterrupted time
2. Access to any existing brand materials
3. Understanding of company history and values
4. Examples of current marketing materials
5. Competitive awareness (who you're NOT)

## Workshop Process

### Phase 1: Brand Voice Discovery (15 minutes)

**1.1 Personality Exploration**

Ask these questions:

- "If your brand was a person at a party, how would they act?"
- "What three words would customers use to describe you?"
- "What tone do you want in every interaction?"

**1.2 Adjective Selection**

From the discussion, identify 3-5 adjectives that capture brand personality:

- Must be specific (not "professional" but "confidently professional")
- Should guide creative decisions
- Need to differentiate from competitors
- Must feel authentic to company culture

**1.3 Voice Validation**

Test selected adjectives:

- Write sample headlines in this voice
- Create an email opening
- Draft a social media post
- Does it feel authentic? If not, refine.

### Phase 2: Unique Mechanism Discovery (20 minutes)

**2.1 Differentiation Exploration**

Key questions to uncover your mechanism:

- "What process or method do you use that competitors don't?"
- "What's your 'secret sauce' for delivering results?"
- "If you had to teach someone your approach, what would be unique?"
- "What do customers say you do differently?"

**2.2 Mechanism Articulation**

Structure your unique mechanism:

- Give it a memorable name
- Explain how it works (3-5 steps)
- Identify why it's different/better
- List specific customer benefits

**2.3 Proof Point Collection**

Gather evidence:

- Customer testimonials mentioning your approach
- Results that demonstrate effectiveness
- Process elements competitors can't replicate
- Patents, proprietary methods, or unique expertise

### Phase 3: Value Proposition Development (15 minutes)

**3.1 Core Promise Identification**

Answer fundamental questions:

- "What transformation do we enable?"
- "What specific problem do we solve?"
- "What outcome do we guarantee?"
- "Why should someone choose us?"

**3.2 Value Prop Formula**

Use this structure:

```
For [target customer]
Who [has this problem/need]
Our [product/service]
Is the [category/solution]
That [unique benefit]
Unlike [alternatives]
We [unique mechanism/approach]
```

**3.3 Supporting Propositions**

Develop 2-3 supporting value props:

- Functional value (what it does)
- Emotional value (how it feels)
- Social value (what it says about them)

### Phase 4: Brand Story Elements (10 minutes)

**4.1 Origin Story**

- Why was the company started?
- What problem needed solving?
- What passion drove the founder(s)?

**4.2 Mission & Vision**

- What change do you seek to make?
- What does success look like?
- How will the world be different?

**4.3 Core Values**

- What 3 principles guide decisions?
- What will you never compromise on?
- What behaviors do you reward?

## Facilitation Tips

### For Best Results:

- Encourage honest, authentic responses
- Push beyond generic answers
- Use "Yes, and..." thinking
- Record everything, edit later
- Look for patterns and themes

### Common Pitfalls:

- Trying to appeal to everyone
- Using industry jargon
- Copying competitor positioning
- Being aspirational vs. authentic
- Overthinking initial responses

## Quality Validation

After workshop completion:

1. Can team members consistently apply the voice?
2. Does the unique mechanism feel truly unique?
3. Do value props resonate with target customers?
4. Is everything authentic to who you are?

## Next Steps

With workshop complete:

1. Document findings in `company-foundation-brief-tmpl.yaml`
2. Share with all team members
3. Create brand guidelines
4. Test with customer feedback
5. Refine based on market response
