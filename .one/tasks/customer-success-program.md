# Customer Success Program Task

This task guides the creation of comprehensive post-purchase experience optimization systems that ensure customer value realization, retention, and advocacy, implementing the UNDERSTAND step of the Elevate Framework's GROW level.

## Purpose

Create systematic customer success programs to:

- Maximize customer value realization and satisfaction through structured onboarding
- Proactively identify and resolve potential issues before they lead to churn
- Build long-term relationships that drive retention and lifetime value growth
- Convert satisfied customers into active advocates and referral sources
- Gather actionable feedback for continuous product and service improvement
- Create predictable growth through expansion and upsell opportunities

## Prerequisites

Before developing customer success programs:

1. Customer journey mapping completed from purchase to advocacy
2. Success metrics and key milestones defined for customer outcomes
3. Customer health scoring framework established
4. CRM and customer data analytics tools configured
5. Support and communication channels integrated
6. Feedback collection systems operational
7. Team roles and responsibilities clarified
8. Onboarding resources and materials prepared

## Common Success Program Pitfalls

1. **Reactive vs. Proactive Approach**: Waiting for problems instead of preventing them
2. **Generic Success Plans**: One-size-fits-all approach ignoring customer uniqueness
3. **Insufficient Health Monitoring**: Missing early warning signs of dissatisfaction
4. **Poor Onboarding Experience**: Overwhelming or inadequate initial customer experience
5. **Lack of Value Communication**: Failing to regularly demonstrate and quantify impact
6. **Inadequate Team Training**: Insufficient skill development for customer success roles
7. **Technology Over-Reliance**: Substituting automation for human relationship building
8. **Short-Term Focus**: Optimizing for immediate metrics vs. long-term relationship value
9. **Siloed Operations**: Lack of integration with sales, support, and product teams
10. **Measurement Without Action**: Collecting data without implementing improvements
