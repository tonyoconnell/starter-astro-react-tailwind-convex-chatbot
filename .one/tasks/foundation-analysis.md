# Foundation Analysis Task

This task guides the comprehensive analysis of your business foundation using the Elevate Framework's three pillars: Company, Market, and Customer. This foundation work is essential before moving to the execution levels (ATTRACT, CONVERT, GROW).

## Purpose

Establish a rock-solid foundation by gaining clarity on:

- **Company**: Your brand voice, unique mechanism, and core offer
- **Market**: Competitive landscape, trends, and positioning opportunities
- **Customer**: Deep avatar understanding including pains, goals, and beliefs

## Prerequisites

Before starting foundation analysis:

1. Access to brand guidelines or existing brand materials
2. List of main competitors (3-5)
3. Customer feedback, reviews, or survey data
4. Basic understanding of your core offer/product

## Process

### Step 1: Company Context Analysis

**1.1 Initiate Brand Workshop**

- Handoff to `brand-identity-specialist` (Marcus Rivera)
- Execute `company-context-workshop` task
- Define brand voice (3-5 adjectives)
- Identify unique mechanism
- Craft value propositions

**1.2 Document Company Foundation**

- Use `company-foundation-brief-tmpl.yaml`
- Include:
  - Brand voice definition
  - Unique mechanism description
  - Core offer details
  - Value proposition statements
  - Brand story elements

### Step 2: Market Intelligence Gathering

**2.1 Initiate Market Research**

- Handoff to `market-research-analyst` (Jennifer Walsh)
- Execute `market-intelligence-research` task
- Analyze 3-5 key competitors
- Identify market trends
- Find positioning gaps

**2.2 Document Market Analysis**

- Use `market-analysis-report-tmpl.yaml`
- Include:
  - Competitive landscape overview
  - Positioning opportunities
  - Market trends affecting your space
  - SWOT analysis
  - Strategic recommendations

### Step 3: Customer Avatar Development

**3.1 Initiate Customer Research**

- Handoff to `customer-journey-designer` (Alex Thompson)
- Execute `customer-avatar-development` task
- Map customer pains and frustrations
- Identify goals and aspirations
- Understand beliefs and worldview

**3.2 Document Customer Profile**

- Use `customer-avatar-profile-tmpl.yaml`
- Include:
  - Demographics and psychographics
  - Pain points (ranked by intensity)
  - Goals and desired outcomes
  - Beliefs and decision criteria
  - Buying behavior patterns

### Step 4: Strategic Alignment Synthesis

**4.1 Integration Workshop**

- Return to orchestrator (Sarah Chen)
- Execute `strategic-alignment-synthesis` task
- Connect insights from all three pillars
- Identify strategic priorities
- Create unified go-to-market approach

**4.2 Document Strategic Blueprint**

- Use `strategic-alignment-blueprint-tmpl.yaml`
- Include:
  - Foundation summary (Company + Market + Customer)
  - Strategic priorities for each Elevate level
  - Resource allocation recommendations
  - Success metrics and KPIs
  - Next steps for execution

## Quality Validation

After completing all foundation work:

1. Execute `foundation-completeness-checklist.md`
2. Ensure minimum 4-star rating achieved
3. Address any gaps identified
4. Get stakeholder approval before proceeding

## Outputs

1. **Company Foundation Brief** - Complete brand identity documentation
2. **Market Analysis Report** - Competitive intelligence and positioning
3. **Customer Avatar Profile** - Deep customer understanding
4. **Strategic Alignment Blueprint** - Integrated strategy document

## Success Criteria

- All three foundation pillars thoroughly analyzed
- Clear strategic direction established
- Team alignment on brand voice and positioning
- Customer insights validate market approach
- Ready to proceed to ATTRACT level implementation

## Next Steps

With foundation complete, proceed to:

1. Level 1: ATTRACT (Hook → Gift → Identify)
2. Level 2: CONVERT (Engage → Sell → Nurture)
3. Level 3: GROW (Upsell → Understand → Share)

## Integration Notes

This task serves as the critical first step in the Elevate Framework. Without solid foundation work, execution efforts lack strategic direction and consistency. The insights gathered here inform every subsequent marketing decision and campaign.

- **C**ontext: Integrate all foundation data across analyses
