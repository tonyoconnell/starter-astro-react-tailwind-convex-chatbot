# Advocacy System Design Task

This task guides the creation of comprehensive referral and sharing systems that transform satisfied customers into active advocates, implementing the SHARE step of the Elevate Framework's GROW level.

## Purpose

Design advocacy systems to:

- Convert satisfied customers into active referral generators
- Create viral sharing mechanisms that amplify reach organically
- Build sustainable community-driven growth engines
- Incentivize and reward advocacy behavior systematically
- Track and optimize viral coefficients for exponential growth
- Generate predictable revenue through referral channels

## Prerequisites

Before developing advocacy systems:

1. Customer success program operational with high satisfaction scores
2. CRM system configured for referral tracking and attribution
3. Customer segmentation and champion identification completed
4. Legal compliance framework for incentive programs established
5. Content and resource library for sharing prepared
6. Analytics infrastructure for viral coefficient measurement
7. Customer feedback systems active for advocacy readiness assessment
8. Brand guidelines and messaging consistency protocols implemented

## Advocacy Program Strategy Framework

### Phase 1: Advocacy Readiness Assessment and Champion Identification (75 minutes)

**1.1 Customer Advocacy Maturity Model**

Evaluate customer readiness for advocacy participation:

**Champion Customer Profile:**

```
Satisfaction Indicators:
- NPS score >9 (Promoter level)
- Customer health score >90
- Consistent product usage (top 20%)
- Support interaction quality (minimal tickets)
- Renewal history (on-time payments)
- Expansion behavior (upsells/cross-sells)

Engagement Indicators:
- Community participation active
- Educational content consumption high
- Feedback provision regular and constructive
- Reference program participation willing
- Social media engagement positive
- Internal adoption advocacy demonstrated

Influence Indicators:
- Industry recognition or thought leadership
- Large social media following
- Speaking engagements or content creation
- Network connections with target prospects
- Decision-making authority in organization
- Peer respect and credibility established
```

**1.2 Advocacy Potential Scoring**

**Multi-Factor Advocacy Score Calculation:**

```
Satisfaction Score (30%):
- NPS rating: 40%
- CSAT scores: 30%
- Product usage depth: 30%

Influence Score (40%):
- Social media reach: 25%
- Industry connections: 25%
- Content creation activity: 25%
- Professional network size: 25%

Engagement Score (30%):
- Community participation: 35%
- Feedback provision: 25%
- Educational content consumption: 20%
- Brand interaction frequency: 20%

Total Advocacy Score = (Satisfaction × 0.3) + (Influence × 0.4) + (Engagement × 0.3)
```

### Phase 6: Viral Coefficient Optimization (60 minutes)

**6.1 Viral Coefficient Measurement Framework**

**Mathematical Model Definition:**

```
Basic Viral Coefficient Calculation:
K = (Number of invitations sent by each user) × (Conversion rate of invitations)

Advanced Multi-Touch Model:
K = Σ(Channel_i × Invitations_i × Conversion_i × Value_i)

Where:
- Channel_i = Channel effectiveness weight
- Invitations_i = Invitations sent per channel
- Conversion_i = Channel conversion rate
- Value_i = Channel-specific value multiplier

Target Viral Coefficient: K > 1.0 (sustainable viral growth)
Excellent Performance: K > 1.5 (exponential expansion)
```

**Coefficient Component Analysis:**

```
Invitation Rate Factors:
- Advocate satisfaction level (NPS correlation)
- Sharing tool accessibility and ease
- Incentive structure attractiveness
- Content quality and relevance
- Timing and context optimization

Conversion Rate Factors:
- Message personalization and relevance
- Social proof and credibility indicators
- Value proposition clarity and strength
- Trust and relationship quality
- Follow-up and nurturing effectiveness
```

## Common Advocacy Program Pitfalls

1. **Insufficient Advocate Preparation**: Launching before customers are truly satisfied
2. **Generic Reward Structures**: One-size-fits-all incentives that don't motivate
3. **Poor Tracking and Attribution**: Inability to measure and optimize performance
4. **Lack of Community Value**: Focusing on extraction rather than value creation
5. **Inadequate Support and Training**: Leaving advocates without tools and guidance
6. **Slow Reward Fulfillment**: Delayed gratification reduces motivation and trust
7. **Limited Sharing Tools**: Making it difficult for advocates to share effectively
8. **Ignoring Feedback**: Not listening to advocate suggestions and concerns
9. **Over-Automation**: Removing human connection and relationship building
10. **Short-Term Focus**: Optimizing for immediate results vs. long-term relationships

## Success Metrics and Industry Benchmarks

### Advocacy Program Performance

- Advocate participation rate: 15-25% of eligible customers
- Referral conversion rate: 20-35% of referrals become customers
- Viral coefficient: 1.2-2.0 for sustainable viral growth
- Advocate retention rate: 80-90% year-over-year
- Program ROI: 300-500% return on investment

### Community Engagement Standards

- Monthly active participation: 60-80% of community members
- Content sharing frequency: 2-4 shares per member per month
- Peer-to-peer interaction rate: 40-60% cross-member engagement
- Knowledge base contribution: 20-30% members creating content
- Event attendance rate: 50-70% participation in community events

### Revenue Impact Targets

- Referral revenue percentage: 10-25% of total new revenue
- Customer lifetime value increase: 20-40% for referred customers
- Sales cycle reduction: 25-50% shorter for referred prospects
- Customer acquisition cost reduction: 30-60% for referral channel
- Expansion revenue correlation: 15-30% higher in referred accounts

Remember: The most successful advocacy programs create genuine value for advocates first, building trust and relationships that naturally lead to referrals and organic growth. Focus on community, recognition, and mutual benefit rather than transactional reward systems.
