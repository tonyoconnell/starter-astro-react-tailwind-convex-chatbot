- [ ] **Idea represents significant innovation or breakthrough** (Required: 4.5+ stars for mission spawning)

  - Innovation scope justifies mission-level investment

- [ ] **Market opportunity is substantial and measurable** (Required: 4.5+ stars for mission spawning)
  - Large addressable market identified
  - Competitive advantage clearly defined
  - Revenue/impact potential quantified
  - Market timing is optimal

- [ ] **Technical innovation is achievable and scalable** (Required: 4+ stars)
  - Technical feasibility confirmed
  - Scalability potential validated
  - Implementation pathway identified
  - Risk factors manageable

- [ ] **Strategic value transcends single story scope** (Required: 4.5+ stars for mission spawning)
  - Impact extends beyond immediate story
  - Organizational transformation potential
  - Long-term strategic advantage
  - Mission-level resource allocation justified

## 🚀 Idea Quality Gate Decision Matrix

### Scoring Calculation
```
Total Weighted Score = 
  (Innovation Potential Score × 0.35) + 
  (Feasibility Assessment Score × 0.25) + 
  (Strategic Value Score × 0.25) + 
  (Resource Viability Score × 0.15)
```

### Quality Gate Thresholds
- **🟢 MISSION SPAWN (4.5+ stars)**: Automatic mission creation triggered
- **🟡 ENHANCED STORY (4.0-4.4 stars)**: Proceed as high-priority story
- **🟢 REGULAR STORY (3.5-3.9 stars)**: Standard story development
- **🔴 REFINEMENT REQUIRED (<3.5 stars)**: Return for idea enhancement
