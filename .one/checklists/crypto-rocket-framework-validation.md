# Crypto R.O.C.K.E.T. Framework Validation Checklist

## Overview

This checklist ensures every chapter meets the R.O.C.K.E.T. framework standards for professional crypto marketing content quality. All chapters must score 7+/10 to progress, with 8+/10 required for final publication.

**R.O.C.K.E.T. Framework**:

- **R**ole - Clear definition and expertise demonstration
- **O**bjective - Specific, measurable outcomes
- **C**ontext - Relevant crypto market and community background
- **K**ey Instructions - Actionable, immediately implementable tactics
- **E**xamples - Real case studies with specific metrics
- **T**one - Crypto-native conversational expertise

## Scoring System

**10 = Exceptional**: Industry-leading quality, viral potential, immediate implementation value  
**9 = Excellent**: Superior quality exceeding professional standards  
**8 = Very Good**: Professional book quality, ready for publication  
**7 = Good**: Acceptable quality, may need minor refinements  
**6 = Fair**: Needs improvement before progression  
**5 = Poor**: Major revisions required

**Minimum Standards**: 7+/10 for phase progression, 8+/10 for final publication
