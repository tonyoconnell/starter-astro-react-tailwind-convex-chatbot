# Brand Alignment Checklist

## Brand Voice Consistency

### ✅ Tone and Style
- [ ] Writing tone matches established brand personality
- [ ] Vocabulary level aligns with target audience sophistication
- [ ] Sentence structure reflects brand communication style
- [ ] Humor, formality, and energy levels are brand-appropriate
- [ ] Technical depth matches audience expertise expectations

### ✅ Brand Personality Expression
- [ ] Content reflects core brand personality traits
- [ ] Examples and analogies align with brand values
- [ ] Problem-solving approach matches brand methodology
- [ ] Leadership positioning is consistent with brand authority
- [ ] Innovation level reflects brand's market position

### ✅ Communication Patterns
- [ ] Information delivery style matches brand approach
- [ ] Persuasion techniques align with brand ethics
- [ ] Storytelling style reflects brand narrative approach
- [ ] Call-to-action language consistent with brand voice
- [ ] Professional boundaries maintained per brand guidelines

## Target Audience Alignment

### ✅ Customer Avatar Consistency
- [ ] Content directly addresses established customer personas
- [ ] Pain points referenced match avatar-specific challenges
- [ ] Solutions align with avatar capabilities and resources
- [ ] Language complexity appropriate for avatar sophistication
- [ ] Examples resonate with avatar professional context

### ✅ Psychological Positioning
- [ ] Content tone matches customer psychology profile
- [ ] Skepticism level addressed appropriately for audience
- [ ] Evidence requirements meet customer proof standards
- [ ] Decision-making process aligns with customer behavior
- [ ] Risk tolerance reflected in implementation approaches

### ✅ Professional Context
- [ ] Industry terminology used correctly and appropriately
- [ ] Professional challenges accurately represented
- [ ] Regulatory or compliance considerations addressed
- [ ] Competitive landscape references are accurate
- [ ] Market dynamics understanding demonstrated

### ✅ Company Identity Consistency
- [ ] Brand mission and values reflected in content approach
